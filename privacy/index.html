ClutterCut

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 1, 2026

The short version

ClutterCut helps you organize your home and remembers where your things are. To do that it uses your camera, your microphone when you talk to Parpar, and what you tell it about your things.

Camera frames and your voice are processed live and are not kept. We don’t sell your data, we don’t use it for advertising, and you can export or delete all of it from Profile → Account.

ClutterCut is operated by Shaan Kumar, West Windsor, New Jersey, USA (“we”, “us”).

What we store

Your account: email address, display name, and your chosen Parpar personality. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never visible to us.

Your home memory: room names, the names of things Parpar remembers, the names of boxes and places, and where they are. This is kept on your phone and backed up to your account so it survives a new phone.

Item photos: when you photograph a pile to have Parpar remember what’s in it, the photo and the cropped picture of each item are stored privately in your account. Only you can access them. Original pile photos are currently kept until you delete your account; we plan to delete them automatically a few days after processing.

Session history (which room, when, and how it ended) is kept on your phone.

Usage records needed to run the service fairly: when voice calls start and how long they last, and how many photos were analyzed. These contain no audio, no transcripts, and no images.

What we don’t store

Camera frames Parpar looks at to understand a room are analyzed and discarded. Recent frames are held only in your phone’s memory while a session is open, so Parpar can show you what he means.

Voice audio is streamed live and is not recorded. What you say is turned into text to understand you; that text is not saved by ClutterCut.

Services that process your data for us

Supabase — accounts, database, and private photo storage.

Google — Gemini analyzes room photos and helps understand what you say; Google Cloud Text-to-Speech creates Parpar’s voice.

Deepgram — converts your speech to text during voice sessions.

Daily — the live audio connection for voice sessions.

Fly.io — runs the voice service.

PostHog — product analytics: which screens are opened, app launches, and crash reports. No session recordings, no screen contents, and no names of your things or rooms.

These services process data only to provide ClutterCut and under their own security and privacy terms.

Your choices and rights

Export: Profile → Account → Export my data gives you a copy of everything ClutterCut holds about you.

Delete: Profile → Account → Delete Account permanently deletes your account, your rooms and things, your photos, and your usage records. Signing out also removes your data from that phone.

Voice and camera are optional per session: you can use Chat instead of voice, and you choose when to start a session.

Questions or requests: clawzbotz@gmail.com.

Children

ClutterCut is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change how we handle your data, we will update this policy and tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
