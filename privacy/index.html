Privacy Policy

Last updated October 8, 2026

The short version

ClutterCut helps you organize your home and remembers where your things are. To do that it uses your camera, your microphone when you talk to Parpar, and what you tell it about your things.

Camera frames and your voice are processed live and are not kept. We don’t sell your data, we don’t use it for advertising, it isn’t used to train AI models, and you can export or delete all of it from Profile → Account.

ClutterCut is operated by Shaan Kumar, West Windsor, New Jersey, USA (“we”, “us”).

What we store

Your account: email address, display name, and your chosen Parpar personality. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never visible to us.

Your home memory: room names, the names of things Parpar remembers, the names of boxes and places, and where they are. This is kept on your phone and backed up to your account so it survives a new phone.

Item photos: when you photograph a pile to have Parpar remember what’s in it, the photo and the cropped picture of each item are stored privately in your account. Only you can access them. Original pile photos are currently kept until you delete your account; we plan to delete them automatically a few days after processing.

Session history: for sessions you finish, which room, when it started and ended, and how many minutes you spent, saved to your account so your bamboo grove (one stalk per week you organize) survives a new phone. The full step-by-step session record stays on your phone for 7 days.

Usage records needed to run the service fairly: when voice calls start and how long they last, and how many photos were analyzed. These contain no audio, no transcripts, and no images.

Your answer about AI services (below): whether you agreed, and when.

What we don’t store

Camera frames Parpar looks at to understand a room are analyzed and discarded. Recent frames are held only in your phone’s memory while a session is open, so Parpar can show you what he means.

Voice audio is streamed live and is not recorded. What you say is turned into text to understand you; that text is not saved by ClutterCut.

AI services, and asking first

Parpar runs on outside AI services: Google Gemini looks at photos of your space and understands what you say or type, Deepgram turns your speech into text, and Google Cloud Text-to-Speech gives Parpar his voice.

Before your first session, the app names these services, explains what each one receives, and asks your permission. Nothing is sent to them until you agree. You can turn this off anytime in Profile → Account → AI Services; your next session will ask again before anything is sent.

These services process your data only to answer each request, and they don’t use it to train their models. Google may keep requests for a limited time solely to detect abuse of its service.

Services that process your data for us

Supabase — accounts, database, and private photo storage.

Google — Gemini analyzes room photos and helps understand what you say; Google Cloud Text-to-Speech creates Parpar’s voice.

Deepgram — converts your speech to text during voice sessions.

Daily — the live audio connection for voice sessions.

Fly.io — runs the voice service.

PostHog — product analytics: which screens are opened, app launches, and crash reports, under a random ID that isn’t linked to your account. No session recordings, no screen contents, and no names of your things or rooms.

These services process data only to provide ClutterCut and under their own security and privacy terms. Our services run in the United States, so your data is processed there.

How long we keep it

Your account, home memory, item photos, session history, and usage records stay until you delete them or your account.

When you delete your account, it is removed from our live systems right away. Copies may remain in our providers’ routine backups for a short time until they are overwritten. Analytics events can’t be traced back to you, so they aren’t part of account deletion.

Keeping it safe

Data travels over encrypted connections, your photos are stored privately so only your account can open them, and our database lets each account read only its own records. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach ever affects your data, we will tell you as the law requires.

Your choices and rights

Export: Profile → Account → Export my data gives you a copy of everything ClutterCut holds about you.

Delete: Profile → Account → Delete Account permanently deletes your account, your rooms and things, your photos, and your usage records. Signing out also removes your data from that phone.

Delete without the app: https://shaanbeta.github.io/cluttercut-legal/delete-account/

Voice and camera are optional per session: you can use Chat instead of voice, and you choose when to start a session.

Depending on where you live (for example California or New Jersey), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or get a copy of your data, and to opt out of its sale, targeted advertising, or profiling. We don’t sell your data, use it for targeted advertising, or profile you. To make a request, use the controls above or email us; we answer within 45 days. If we turn a request down, you can appeal by replying, and we will answer the appeal within 45 days.

Questions or requests: clawzbotz@gmail.com.

Children

ClutterCut is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change how we handle your data, we will update this policy and tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
