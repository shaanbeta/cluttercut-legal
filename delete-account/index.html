Delete your ClutterCut account

Last updated October 8, 2026

In the app

Open ClutterCut, go to Profile → Account, and tap Delete Account. Your account is deleted right away.

Without the app

Email clawzbotz@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my account”. We reply to confirm it’s really you, then delete your account within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What gets deleted

Your account and profile, your rooms, the things Parpar remembers and where they are, your item photos, your session history, and your usage records.

What may remain

Copies in our providers’ routine backups, for a short time until they are overwritten. Product analytics events use a random ID that isn’t linked to your account, so they can’t be traced back to you.

Before you go

Want a copy of everything first? In the app: Profile → Account → Export my data.
